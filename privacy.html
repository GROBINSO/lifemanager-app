LifeManager privacy policy

Effective 30 September 2026

Home Terms of use

LifeManager is a desktop app that runs on your own Windows computer. This policy explains what it does with your information. In short: it keeps your data on your computer, and the only information it ever handles from Google is what is needed to store your backups in your own Google Drive.

Information stored on your computer

Your tasks, calendar entries, notes, finances and health records are stored in a database file on your computer, encrypted with a key that is protected by your Windows account. They are never sent to the developer, and LifeManager has no servers to send them to. It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking.

Google Drive backups

Connecting a Google account is optional. If you do:

  • LifeManager asks for the drive.file permission only. This lets it create a folder called “LifeManager Backups” in your Google Drive and add, list and delete the backup files that it created itself. It cannot see, open or change any other file in your Drive.
  • It reads your Google account's email address, only so that it can show you which account is connected.
  • The backups it uploads are already encrypted on your computer before they are sent, so Google stores files that it cannot read.
  • The sign-in token Google issues is stored on your computer, inside the encrypted database. It is used only to upload and tidy up your backups.

Information received from Google is used only to provide this backup feature to you. It is not sold, not shared with anyone, not used for advertising, and never leaves your computer except in the requests to Google that the feature needs.

LifeManager's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can disconnect Google Drive at any time in LifeManager (Settings → Backups → Disconnect), which revokes its access with Google, or remove LifeManager from your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Backups already in your Drive stay there until you delete them. Uninstalling LifeManager and deleting its folder removes everything it stored on your computer.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date. Questions can be raised on GitHub.
