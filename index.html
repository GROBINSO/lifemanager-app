LifeManager

A personal desktop app for running day-to-day life, kept on your own computer.

Privacy policy Terms of use

LifeManager is a Windows desktop app for one person's own use. It brings together tasks, a calendar, bills and savings, and health records, with reminders for what is coming up.

Your data stays with you

Everything is stored in a database on your own computer, encrypted. LifeManager has no servers and no accounts, and it sends nothing anywhere unless you ask it to.

Google Drive backups (optional). If you connect a Google account, LifeManager copies its encrypted backups to a “LifeManager Backups” folder in your Google Drive. It asks only for access to the files it creates itself, so it cannot see anything else in your Drive. See the privacy policy for exactly what is used and why.
LifeManager is a personal project, maintained on GitHub by GROBINSO.
